GTA RADAR — MUNICIPAL-LED EVENT INTELLIGENCE

One-time: Extract this folder next to older Radar folders. Run START_RADAR.bat.
For daily scheduling and your encrypted DeepSeek key, run INSTALL_DAILY.bat once.

The system now analyzes municipal records FIRST regardless of missing company/address or LOW relevance. It preserves the raw source DB, deduplicates per source record, enriches from business observations, and keeps pending opportunities.

New outputs (in data folder):
- opportunity_dashboard.html: source-by-source municipal vs business opportunity counts
- collector_opportunity_audit.csv: every source, including zero-recent sources
- opportunities_ALL_REVIEW_ONLY.csv: full recently dated opportunity list
- recent_queue_pending.csv: retained research backlog
- lead_research_dashboard.html: identity/contact research queue

Two-day rolling event window for daily activity. Dates with no parseable event/observed timestamp are preserved in source data but cannot be asserted as 48-hour activity; they are shown in per-source audit as not selected. This release does not itself establish independently verified company operators or confirmed contacts. DeepSeek is optional, USD 0.50 and max 50 daily.

Existing collectors are not erased or rebuilt, old database folders stay intact. Unverified leads are not marked sale-ready.
