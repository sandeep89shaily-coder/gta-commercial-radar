GTA RADAR DAILY — RECENT LEADS UPDATE

What changed:
- START_RADAR now checks records with source EVENT dates within the past 2 days, including records from original baselines.
- The daily unattended run uses the same selector.
- Selection uses source dates, not the day a historical file was downloaded.
- Duplicate source/date combinations are remembered in a separate queue history.
- Recent opportunities stay in the pending queue when AI cannot run; they remain REVIEW ONLY.
- Older records are not incorrectly called fresh leads. Some records without reliable event dates are excluded.

Simple use:
1. Extract this full ZIP into Desktop/new data. It creates GTA_RADAR_DAILY_48H, leaving old folders untouched.
2. Open GTA_RADAR_DAILY_48H and double-click START_RADAR.bat.
3. When ready for automatic daily runs, double-click INSTALL_DAILY.bat once.

Note: Autonomous web discovery still is not enabled without a permitted search service. DeepSeek alone analyzes attached evidence, and missing evidence can mean no paid AI investigations. These are genuine remaining limitations, not collection failures.
