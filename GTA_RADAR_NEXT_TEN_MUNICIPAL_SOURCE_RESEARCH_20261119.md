## Specific schema breakthrough
Ajax official layer lists REF_ID, ARN, ADDRESS, LOCATION, APPLICANT, TYPE, DESCRIPTION, PLANNER/EMAIL_CONTACT, STATUS, SUBMISSION_DATE, SP_APP/SP_DATE_SUBMIT, ZONING_APP/ZONING_DATE_SUBMIT and commercial nonresidential area fields. It also contains 'Pre-consultation' and 'Active' subtype values. Do NOT treat all features or planner emails as distinct projects or buyers.

Newmarket official layer lists FILE_NUM, LOCATION, OWNER, DESCRIPTION, APP_STATUS, PROPOSE_LAND_USE, WEBLINK and separate INDUSTRIAL_FLOOR_AREA_SQM / COMMERCIAL_FLOOR_AREA_SQM. EDIT_WHEN is a revision timestamp; no original publication date proven.

## Acceptance gates before implementation
1. Official permitted source and documented reuse rights checked; query/export allowed, no access bypass.
2. Real bounded sample with ≥20 source rows when available, showing exact unique application identifiers and addresses, robust pagination and no duplicates; do not invent counts from schema pages.
3. Original publication date explicitly separated from date received, GIS edit date and Radar first seen.
4. Current commercial/industrial scope and application stage independently source-backed. Planner and corporate main line NOT automatically a buyer.
5. Existing 24-city collectors and shared DeepSeek ledger remain untouched until a new city clears acceptance. No false citywide completion promises.

**Recommendation:** begin verification with Ajax and Newmarket first, then Windsor/Pickering. Commercial potential alone does not justify switching on unverified feeds. Check today's original Radar run quality before deployment.
