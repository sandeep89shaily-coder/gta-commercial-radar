Your sales team shouldn't have to discover projects after everyone else.

How it works

From public project change to a focused sales lead to investigate.

A clear, repeatable process designed to help supplier teams find relevant work without mistaking permits or licences for confirmed purchases.

01 / MONITOR

Track activity

Public planning, permitting, facility, licensing and industrial records across the GTA.

02 / MATCH

Identify your fit

Map project descriptions to your product, trade or service offering.

03 / REVIEW

Build the brief

Capture location, stage, potential need, source evidence and missing details.

04 / VERIFY

Qualify further

Check project timing, relevant companies and buying authority before outreach.

Radar is an early-signal intelligence service, not a tender marketplace or a promise of available contracts. Specific buyer and procurement details are included only when supported by evidence.
