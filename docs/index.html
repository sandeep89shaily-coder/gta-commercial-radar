The platform

From scattered public records to focused commercial insight.

Radar combines official notices, permits and other public signals, then organizes them around the real question: what work might be coming, where, and who needs to verify it?

⌁

How it works

Built for discovery. Disciplined about certainty.

Four practical stages transform source activity into a focused research workflow, with uncertainty visible at every step.

01 / MONITOR

Watch official sources

Collect public development, business-licensing and industrial notices from the GTA and surrounding regions.

02 / CONNECT

Identify relevant changes

Group records by project, address and source history while avoiding unsupported tenant or buyer matches.

03 / PRIORITIZE

Focus on possible demand

Highlight scope that may imply supplier needs and explain the source stage behind each signal.

04 / VERIFY

Confirm before outreach

Check current project status, decision-makers and whether any opportunity is actually open to suppliers.
