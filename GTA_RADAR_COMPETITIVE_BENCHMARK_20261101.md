# GTA Radar — first ten-project competitor benchmark (public evidence only)

**Audit date:** 2026-11-01 (America/Toronto). **Scope:** The exact ten permit project IDs frozen in [config/google_pilot_reviewed_projects_20261101.txt](config/google_pilot_reviewed_projects_20261101.txt). **Status: initial external evidence audit; NOT a controlled performance head-to-head.** No competitor paid logins, API subscriptions, private dashboards, or live collection logs were accessed. Do not silently substitute different projects.

## Executive finding

**There is no defensible evidence that GTA Radar discovered any of the selected ten projects before GROUND, Datum, or DataBid.** Publicly available information for some projects predates the selected permit events; that is not the same thing as competitor discovery, but it rules out public-information novelty. **GROUND publicly exposes one exact address from the cohort, 8 Prince Arthur Avenue.** The other nine have *unverified competitor coverage*—not confirmed absence.

Important distinctions:
- **First public disclosure** is not the same as an application/permit source date.
- **Radar first observed** must be taken from its persisted ingestion ledger, not inferred from the 2026-11-01 shortlist or from a 2025/2026 application date.
- **Competitor first observed** must be proved by dated records, independently timestamped updates, or access-permitted exports—not assumed from a website currently showing an address.
- Matching an **address** does not establish the same **project or permit**; some addresses carry unrelated permits.
- Having an architect/occupant/owner name does not establish a real buying authority or open supplier package.
- A paid customer may value a later **new work package** even if the underlying address was known years earlier; only claim new actionable value with evidence.

## Required next steps — fixed acceptance gates, not a moving feature roadmap

1. **Original record truth:** Recheck all ten exact permit IDs in Toronto's [official building-permit status search](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/building-construction/building-permit/after-you-apply-for-a-building-permit/search-the-status-of-a-building-permit-application/); separate exact permit from child/other address-level permits and attach date-stamped evidence.
2. **Timeline truth:** Retrieve Radar's persisted `first_seen_local` (or source observation timestamp) from its own original Nov 1 daily CSV/ledger; capture first publicly provable municipality posting time, separately from application receipt date. If missing, mark **UNKNOWN**, never retroactively infer detection from source date.
3. **Competitor comparison:** On each project's *same exact permit/project* record, manually use permitted public/preview/trial access to compare GROUND, Datum and DataBid. Capture publicly shown record and dated first-seen/add time, if provided; mark unknown otherwise. Record match level (**address only**, **same permit**, **same project**) separately.
4. **Customer-fit truth:** Identify project sponsor and lawful public business contact; distinguish owner/developer/architect/operator; find whether the particular matched service package is contracted, unknown or still open **only if sourced**.
5. **Commercial proof:** Ask specialty suppliers if a short evidence-linked dossier revealed useful, new information. Report no paid pilot customer until actual payment is confirmed.

### Proposed benchmark pass/fail criteria for future controlled cohorts

- **Accuracy:** 100% exact-ID traceability and source link for every *published* intelligence claim; unknown is acceptable, invented claims are not.
- **Detection:** Report actual ingestion/publish timestamps; compare only same-project milestones, with sample sizes and missing competitor timestamps disclosed.
- **Relevance:** Test whether manually reviewed trade matches correctly correspond to explicit source scope, including false-positive rate and duplicates.
- **Actionability:** Do not call a lead open/unawarded unless supplier selection and buyer authority are independently established.
- **Customer value:** At least two customers pay for pilot intelligence, report what was useful, and have a clear opt-out from future outreach.

**Strategic positioning:** Aspire to Canada's leading evidence-linked construction and commercial-development intelligence service. A small manual pilot validates market value but does not constrain the product's eventual scale. No changes to scheduled collection, DeepSeek, Google pilot or databases are made by this research document.
