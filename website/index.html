05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect.

Are these confirmed projects or open bids?

Not always. Reports can include early research candidates. We clearly distinguish original-source facts, unresolved project identity, and procurement that has not been verified.

Is this just a list of building permits?

No. The research also considers planning activity, industrial filings, council documents and company context. Each source has its own limitations.

How current is the information?

We separately track source-record dates and ComIntel observation dates when available. The date an application was received is not necessarily the date it first became public.

Can I get a sample for my industry?

Yes. Choose a sector and area below. We'll discuss the available research format and suitable examples; current availability depends on source verification.
