## Implemented in this patch

2. **Fulltext discovery:** update official Invest Ontario and Martinrea index URLs; fairly share the bounded fulltext retrieval budget among publisher indexes. Failed sources remain FAILED, not converted into live sources.
3. **Council PDF bridge:** recognize the real `TOTAL_INTELLIGENCE_REVIEW/MEETING_DOCUMENTS/Toronto/COMBINED_PDF_FETCH_AUDIT.csv` and automatic-audit paths from this ZIP, verify source host and SHA256 before extracting any text, and keep findings as review-only mentions.
4. **Authorized local CSV:** extend SHA256/provenance-checked offline import to Brampton and Mississauga. Require affirmative licence/permission documentation for those cities; keep all imports research-only.

## Human/operator actions before calling access 'recovered'

- Obtain dataset-specific permitted export + evidence of reuse rights, save a dated untouched CSV and official source URL.
- Put the CSV at `GTA_RADAR_AUTHORIZED_EXPORTS/<citylower>.csv` and create/update `manifest.json` with `permitted_public_export=true`, `commercial_reuse_permission_confirmed=true`, `dataset_licence`, `licence_or_permission_url`, SHA256 and explicit `columns` mappings.
- Do **not** mark `commercial_reuse_permission_confirmed` merely because a website has a download button. Human review of source-specific terms is necessary. Do not send a municipality or publisher email without operator approval.
- Keep robots-denied endpoints blocked. Do not proxy, alter identity, disable TLS, or use third-party caches to circumvent restrictions.
- After a permitted source works, compare stable application IDs and actual source dates; no backlog publication timestamps are inferred. Treat all new import rows as baseline until a valid change is observed.
