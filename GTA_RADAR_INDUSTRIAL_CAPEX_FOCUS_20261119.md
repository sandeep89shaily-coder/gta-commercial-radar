# GTA Radar — Industrial investment and equipment procurement focus (2026-11-19)

## Product decision
Primary product is now **industrial capital expenditure intelligence** for machinery vendors, plant integrators, automation vendors, industrial utilities, refrigeration/process cooling, electrical infrastructure and equipment-service firms. Existing municipal collectors continue as source inputs; do not prioritize geographic expansion, generic residential construction, broad tender directories, website polish or generalized AI additions until the industrial evidence gates below pass.

## Evidence ladder (never collapse)
1. **Funded or announced project:** exact company, scope, source link and date; investment dollars are not equipment spend.
2. **Equipment requirement:** explicit named equipment or evidence-backed engineering need. Facility-only proposals are **not** proven machinery orders.
3. **Procurement stage:** verify if design/specification/vendor selection/RFQ has occurred and whether work is already awarded.
4. **Buyer identity:** source-backed company role, responsible project lead, verified professional contact route; business sales inbox is not an equipment purchaser.
5. **Opportunity release:** prove work is prospective and relevant to a specific supplier; no sales-ready labels from AI inference.
